# Metasploitable 2

This section is for notes from authorized practice with the intentionally vulnerable Metasploitable 2 virtual machine.

## Lab record

When adding an exercise, record:

- The isolated lab setup and target address
- The service or vulnerability investigated
- The commands and observations needed to reproduce the result
- The security impact and appropriate remediation

## Safety

Keep the VM on an isolated training network. Do not scan or test systems outside the lab or without explicit authorization. Do not include credentials or sensitive host information in this repository.
